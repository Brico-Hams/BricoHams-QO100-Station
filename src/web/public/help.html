BricoHams QO-100: quick help

  1. Sign in with the Google account the station owner approved.
  2. Choose a mode, then click the spectrum to tune. Arrow keys tune too (Shift = 10 steps).
  3. Press Listen to hear the receiver.
  4. To transmit (operators only): Arm TX, allow the microphone, then hold PTT or the Space key. Only one operator can hold the turn.
  5. The carrier drops automatically on time-out (TOT), lost connection or release.
  6. The beacon indicator shows whether the receiver is locked to the QO-100 beacon and how many hertz it corrects.
  7. Log contacts in the Log tab, export ADIF, or upload to LoTW and QRZ.

The full manual (Spanish) is in the docs/help folder of the project.
